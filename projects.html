Exam Grader

University project · Warsaw University of Technology

An end-to-end pipeline that grades handwritten Polish exam papers. A vision-language model transcribes the scans, an LLM evaluates each answer against grading keywords, and uncertain segments are flagged for manual review. A 30-task exam is graded in about 2 minutes with 93% accuracy.

An AI platform for board game events. Upload a game's rulebook and Tripolis generates an interactive summary, styled with colors matching the game, plus an AI agent that explains the rules and answers questions. Players scan a QR code and have everything on their phones.
